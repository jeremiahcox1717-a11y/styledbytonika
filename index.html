Booking Policies

Deposits

A non-refundable $20 deposit is required to secure your appointment. This amount will be applied toward your final balance. Remaining Balance MUST be in Cash only.

Traveling

For mobile services, a travel fee will be added. Rates may vary depending on location.

Delay

We allow a 15-minute grace period. After that, a late fee will apply or your appointment may need to be rescheduled.

Reschedule

Need to reschedule? Please notify us at least 48 hours in advance so we can accommodate you and offer your spot to another client.
