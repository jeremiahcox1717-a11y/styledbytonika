Booking Policies

Deposits

A non-refundable $25 deposit is required to secure your appointment. This amount will be applied toward your final balance. Remaining balance MUST be in Cash only.

Traveling

As a mobile service, a travel fee will be added. Rates may vary depending on location.

Delay

We allow a 15-minute grace period. After that, a late fee will apply or your appointment may need to be rescheduled.

Reschedule

Need to reschedule? Please notify us at least 48 hours in advance so we can accommodate you and offer your spot to another client.

Before your Appt

Before your appointment, please ensure you're ready to begin at your scheduled time. This allows me to dedicate the full appointment to you, ensuring a smooth, relaxed, and unhurried experience without any unnecessary delays.

Bring any inspiration photos, reference styles, or ideas you love so we can fully understand your vision and personalize your look exactly to your preference.

Come with clean, dry hair that is free from heavy styling products, oils, or buildup to help us achieve the best possible results and flawless final finish.

If you need to reschedule or cancel your appointment, please notify us in advance to allow us to manage our schedule efficiently and accommodate other valued clients.
